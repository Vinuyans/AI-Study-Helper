12. What are the three groups of Near Earth Asteroids (NEAs)?

- **Atens:** NEAs with semi-major axes smaller than Earth's
- **Apollos:** Earth-crossing NEAs with semi-major axes larger than Earth's
- **Amors:** Earth-approaching NEAs with orbits exterior to Earth's but interior to Mars'

**13. What are Trojan asteroids and where are they located?**

Trojan asteroids are several hundred asteroids located near Jupiter's L4 and L5 Lagrange points, which are 60 degrees ahead and 60 degrees behind Jupiter in its solar orbit.

**14. According to the widely accepted theory, where do long-period comets originate from?**

The Oort Cloud.

**15. What is a comet's coma?**

A surrounding cloud of diffuse material (gas and dust) that usually grows in size and brightness as the comet approaches the Sun.

**16. List 3 changes that occur to a comet as it approaches the Sun.**

- The surface of the nucleus begins to warm
- Volatiles evaporate
- A coma of gas and dust forms
- Dust reflects sunlight
- Gas in the coma absorbs ultraviolet radiation and begins to fluoresce
- A hydrogen envelope forms
- Tails develop (dust tail and ion tail)

**18. Define: meteoroid, meteor, and meteorite.**

- **Meteoroid:** Small solid particles orbiting the Sun
- **Meteor:** A meteoroid that enters Earth's atmosphere and burns up
- **Meteorite:** Any part of a meteor that reaches the ground

**19. What causes meteor showers?**

Meteor showers are caused when Earth passes through meteoroids released from comets as volatiles boil or sublimate from them. These particles become meteors as they enter Earth's atmosphere.
