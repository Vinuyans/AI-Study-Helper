# Scoring

This section explains how scoring works in both PSS10 and GAAIS questionnaires

## PSS10

The answers from questions 4, 5, 7 and 8 are reversed as shown in the following mapping:

<center> 0 = 4, 1 = 3, 2 = 2, 3 = 1, 4 = 0 </center>

The final score is then summed up and can be compared to this table:

|Score Range | Stress Level |
|------------|--------------|
|0-13|Low Stress|
|14-26|Medium Stress|
|27-40|High Stress|

## GAAIS

The GAAIS scale works by having 2 means: the positive attitude towards AI and the negative attitude towards AI. Scoring for the positive scale means that Strongly disagree = 1; Disagree = 2; Neutral = 3; Agree = 4; and Strongly agree = 5 while the negative scale means that Strongly disagree = 5; (Somewhat)
disagree = 4; Neutral = 3; (Somewhat) agree = 2; and Strongly agree = 1. The mean is calculated for each subscale and will be between 1 and 5. Higher scores on both subscale indicates a more positive attitude since a high score on the negative scale shows that the user is likely to forgive AI.
